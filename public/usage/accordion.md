# accordion (component)

Stacked sections that open one at a time, such as the steps of a character builder or a list of advanced settings.

### When to use
- A long set of options groups naturally into sections people rarely need all at once, such as character traits.
- Answers to common questions sit under short questions.

### Reach for instead
- **tabs** — when the sections are peers people switch between often

### Rules
- **Do:** Write short section titles that say what is inside, such as "Lighting" or "Wardrobe". **Don't:** Hide primary settings people need every time inside a closed section.

### Accessibility
- Each trigger is a button announcing expanded or collapsed; Enter or Space toggles it.

### Design tokens
`--divider` · `--muted-foreground`
