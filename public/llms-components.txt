# mantis components

> Every component and block in the registry: what it looks like, when to use it, and where its full usage guide lives.

## Primitives

Installed with the base item; blocks build on them.

- [Icon](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/icon.json) — Google Material Symbols (Rounded, weight 300) as an inline SVG component: `<Icon name="close" />`. Sizes to the surrounding text and paints in its color. Installed by the base item. Use when: A button, menu item or render status needs a small symbol that backs up its label. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/icon.md)
- [Button](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/button.json) — The Mantis button, measured from the reference product: white default (everyday confirm/save), lime brand (the one main call to action: generate, sign up, buy a plan), lime tint, secondary, glass, soft, outline, ghost, destructive, commerce pink and commerce blue looks; flat, raised (chunky CTA edge) and glossy (generate) depths; 24–52px sizes. Hover dims to 80% and press to 60% brightness. Use when: The user is about to change something, such as saving a preset, publishing a generation to the community or deleting an asset. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/button.md)
- [Dialog](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/dialog.json) — A modal window in the reference's .dialog style: #131517, white-4% border, 16px radius, 24px padding, 20px title, 16px description, right-aligned actions, black-80% overlay. Material Symbols close icon. Use when: An action needs confirming before it runs, especially a delete that cannot be undone. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/dialog.md)
- [Dropdown Menu](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/dropdown-menu.json) — A menu of actions opened from a trigger, in the reference's menu style (#1c1e20, white-4% border, 12px radius, 32px items, #23262a hover, gray icons, white-8% dividers), with Material Symbols icons. Use when: A tile, card or toolbar has more actions than fit as buttons, such as upscale, remix and delete on one generation. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/dropdown-menu.md)
- [Toaster](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/sonner.json) — Short, temporary status messages (toasts) — shadcn's sonner, with Material Symbols status icons. This registry maintains the copy. Use when: An action finished where the result is not otherwise visible, such as "Published to community" after publishing from the asset library. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/sonner.md)
- [Badge](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/badge.json) — A short label beside the thing it describes: new (lime on lime 20%), neutral (white on white 20%), tag (solid lime caps), sale (pink caps), hot (pink gradient), value (blue gradient caps) and gold gradient text, measured from the reference. Commerce fills use WCAG-approved shades. Use when: A model, preset or nav item is new and should stand out for a while (`new`), or a plan or allowance needs a lime highlight, such as "Unlimited" or "3 free generations" (`new`, `tag`). [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/badge.md)
- [Tooltip](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/tooltip.json) — A small dark label (#171717, 1px #222 border, 12px medium, 8px radius, no arrow) that names an icon-only control on hover or focus. Measured from the reference. Use when: An icon button in the composer or toolbar has no visible text, such as add media or mention an element. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/tooltip.md)
- [Popover](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/popover.json) — A small floating panel anchored to a control: page-colored, 16px radius, white-4% border, soft shadow, 8px padding. For one short task such as picking a clip length. Use when: A setting chip opens its options right where it sits, such as duration or batch size. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/popover.md)
- [Switch](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/switch.json) — Turns one setting on or off immediately. Default 28×16 track with a 12px thumb, large 36×24 for billing period; lime on, slate off. Measured from the reference. Use when: A single option takes effect the moment it changes, with no save step, such as enhance prompt or audio. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/switch.md)
- [Textarea](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/textarea.json) — Multi-line text: filled field for forms, borderless prompt (14px) and prompt-lg (18px) for composers; grows with content. Use when: Someone writes a prompt in a composer (`prompt` in studio composers, `prompt-lg` in the large hero composer). [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/textarea.md)
- [Input](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/input.json) — Single-line text field measured from the reference: 40px (or 44px lg), white-5% fill, no border, 14px text, lime focus ring. Use when: The answer is short, typed and not from a fixed list, such as a preset name, a seed number or an email to invite. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/input.md)
- [Checkbox](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/checkbox.json) — Pick any number of options. From the reference's own .checkbox definition: 16/18/20/24px, 6px radius, 2px control-border (3:1), lime (brand) or white (primary) when checked. Use when: Several options can be on at once, such as selecting generations to delete or formats to export. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/checkbox.md)
- [Radio Group](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/radio-group.json) — Choose exactly one option: 20px radios matching the checkbox, or whole RadioGroupCard cards with a lime outline and corner check when chosen. Use when: One choice out of two to six options must be made, such as a project being private or public. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/radio-group.md)
- [Toggle](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/toggle.json) — A single pressable button styled like one segment of a toggle group. Use when: One on/off formatting option sits in a toolbar, such as showing the grid overlay on a canvas. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/toggle.md)
- [Toggle Group](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/toggle-group.json) — A compact row of options measured from the reference: segment (selected turns white with dark 12px semibold text) or outline tiles (selected gets a 2px lime outline). Use when: One short option out of a few must be picked inline, such as upscale factor or Visuals versus Sound (`segment`). [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/toggle-group.md)
- [Slider](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/slider.json) — Drag a 12px white thumb along a 4px separator-colored line with no filled range, from the reference's .slider definition. Use when: The exact number matters less than the feel, such as zooming a grid or setting motion strength. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/slider.md)
- [Select](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/select.json) — Composer-style chip (40px, #222, lime-5% border, 60%-white text) that opens the reference menu (#1c1e20, white-4% border, 32px items, #23262a hover) to pick one value. Use when: A setting has more options than fit inline, such as aspect ratio, quality or style. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/select.md)
- [Skeleton](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/skeleton.json) — Dark #202227 placeholder with the reference's 2s vertical shimmer (stops under reduced motion), shaped like the content it stands in for. Use when: A grid of generations or assets is loading and its layout is known in advance. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/skeleton.md)
- [Kbd](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/kbd.json) — A keyboard key such as ⌘K: 18px, 4px radius, white-5% fill, white-50% semibold text, measured from the reference's search pill. Use when: A control has a keyboard shortcut worth teaching, such as ⌘K to open search. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/kbd.md)
- [Separator](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/separator.json) — A 1px white-8% divider between groups inside one surface, from the reference's menu separator. Use when: Two groups inside one card or menu need a quiet break, such as account actions above Sign out. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/separator.md)
- [Avatar](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/avatar.json) — Round photo or initials for a person: 20/24/32/40px, white initials on the inset grey. Use when: A person is shown beside their content, such as a post author or a project owner. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/avatar.md)
- [Progress](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/progress.json) — A thin lime bar on a white-5% track for amounts used or done, such as credits left. Use when: A known amount is partly used, such as 230 of 600 credits. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/progress.md)
- [Heading](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/heading.json) — Page and section headings: uppercase Space Grotesk display levels hero/section/sub/label with measured responsive steps (768px, 1280px), HeadingAccent for one lime word, and Inter title levels for dialogs and panels. Use when: A page or studio opens with a statement (`hero`), optionally with one word in lime (`HeadingAccent`). [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/heading.md)
- [Alert Dialog](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/alert-dialog.json) — Blocking confirmation for irreversible actions, in the reference's .dialog style: #131517, white-4% border, 16px radius, 24px padding, 20px title, black-80% overlay. Use when: The next step destroys data or money and cannot be reversed, such as deleting a project. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/alert-dialog.md)
- [Sheet](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/sheet.json) — Panel that slides in from an edge for details or settings, using the dialog surface, separator border and overlay. Use when: Details of a selected item should appear without leaving the grid, such as a generation's prompt and settings. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/sheet.md)
- [Accordion](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/accordion.json) — Stacked sections that open one at a time, separated by white-8% dividers, with grey chevrons. Use when: A long set of options groups naturally into sections people rarely need all at once, such as character traits. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/accordion.md)
- [Field](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/field.json) — Label, hint and error wrapper for form controls: 12px grey labels above fields, as in the reference's profile and settings forms. Use when: Any form input needs a visible label above it, such as Username or Bio. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/field.md)
- [Tabs](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/tabs.json) — Switch panels in place, in three measured looks: glass bar (default), underline (line) and pill. Use when: One subject has two to six views of equal weight, such as a project's images, videos and characters, and people move between them freely. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/tabs.md)
- [Card](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/card.json) — Groups one subject on a surface, in four measured looks: default solid #1c1e20, glass (white 5%), outlined (#131517 with a faint inner highlight) and media. Use when: Several items of the same kind sit side by side in a grid, such as projects or saved presets, and each needs its own title and actions. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/card.md)
- [Table](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/table.json) — Rows of comparable records such as usage and billing history: white-8% row dividers, 12px grey headers, white-5% hover. Use when: People compare several records on the same fields, such as recent generations, render jobs or credit usage by project. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/table.md)
- [Input Group](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/input-group.json) — An input with icons or one action inside its frame (search magnifier, Copy link), borderless like Input with a lime focus ring. Use when: An input needs a leading icon, such as search. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/input-group.md)
- [Combobox](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/combobox.json) — Searchable picker: borderless field that filters a list in the reference menu style, for long lists such as presets or models. Use when: The list is long enough that scrolling is slow, such as dozens of presets or models. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/combobox.md)
- [Icon Tile](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/icon-tile.json) — Small colored rounded square behind an icon for nav, menu and settings entries, colored from the reference's own palette pairs; decorative. Use when: Entries in a sidebar, settings nav or menu benefit from a quick color cue, such as Profile, Subscription and Usage. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/icon-tile.md)
- [Spinner](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/spinner.json) — Spinning progress ring with an announced label, for busy buttons and queued items. Use when: An action is in progress and its duration is unknown, such as "Upscaling" on a button. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/spinner.md)
- [Stepper](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/stepper.json) — The composer's '− 1/4 +' control for small whole-number settings such as batch size, in a 40px composer chip. Use when: A small count with a known maximum is adjusted one step at a time, such as batch size 1 to 4. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/stepper.md)
- [Media Tile](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/media-tile.json) — Image or video in a rounded frame with an optional uppercase title, corner badges, lime selection ring and hover actions; renders as a button, a real link or a plain frame. For pickers, galleries, feeds and libraries. Use when: A grid or strip shows pictures or clips that people open, pick or act on, such as presets, uploads, past generations or library assets. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/media-tile.md)
- [Pagination](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/pagination.json) — Compact pager for tables: optional rows-per-page picker, a live 'Page X of Y' and named previous and next buttons, in a labelled nav landmark. Use when: A table or list has more rows than fit comfortably, such as a credit history or a render queue, and people step through it a page at a time. [Guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/usage/pagination.md)

## Helpers

- [Mantis cn](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/mantis-cn.json) — The class-name helper every Mantis component imports: shadcn's cn, taught Mantis's text styles (text-display-lg, text-title-sm…) so a color class never silently drops them. Installed by the base item.
- [Mantis agent guide](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/agents.json) — MANTIS.md: the index AI coding agents read before building UI with Mantis (components and their real props, token utilities, rules, links to every usage guide). Installed by the base item at the project root.
- [Sample content](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/sample-content.json) — Invented placeholder data for the page templates: the sample product Lumen, its signed-in account, top bar and footer, image and video models, generations, projects, clips, assets, signed-in devices, credit usage, sidebar sections and canvas layers. Every template falls back to it when you leave its content props out, so it renders with no props at all, like a component with placeholder text. Replace all of it with real data before shipping.

## Blocks

Compositions of primitives. `intent` lists the jobs a block does; `use_when` says when to reach for it.

- [Hello Card](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/hello-card.json) — _[content]_ You are verifying a fresh install of this design system renders correctly.
- [Pricing Card](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/pricing-card.json) — _[commerce, marketing]_ You are showing a paid plan (such as Creator) on a pricing page or upgrade panel, with monthly and yearly prices, what it includes, and a button to subscribe; use one card per plan and badge only the best-value plan.
- [Prompt Composer](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/prompt-composer.json) — _[form]_ You need the place where someone writes a prompt, picks a model, aspect ratio and number of images, and starts an image generation that spends credits.
- [Notification Settings](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/notification-settings.json) — _[settings, form]_ You are building account settings and people need to choose which emails they receive and how often a digest arrives, saved together with one button.
- [Project Card](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/project-card.json) — _[data-display, productivity]_ You are showing someone's projects as a grid they can open, rename, duplicate and delete, such as a studio home or project library.
- [Empty State](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/empty-state.json) — _[feedback]_ A library, gallery or list has finished loading and is empty, such as an asset library before the first upload, or a search returned no results.
- [Model Picker](https://mantisdesignsystem.com/r/model-picker.json) — _[form, navigation]_ You let people choose which AI model runs a generation and each model needs a one-line description to choose by, such as the model chip in a composer or the model row in a studio rail

## Intent vocabulary

- `auth` — Authentication and account access — sign-in, sign-up, recovery, verification.
- `form` — Structured data entry and validation.
- `data-display` — Presenting records, lists, and metrics for reading.
- `data-viz` — Charts and quantitative visuals.
- `navigation` — Moving around an app — bars, breadcrumbs, tabs, command menus.
- `app-shell` — Full-page layout scaffolding for application screens.
- `feedback` — System status — alerts, notifications, empty and error states.
- `marketing` — Landing-page and promotional sections.
- `commerce` — Purchase, checkout, and billing.
- `messaging` — Conversational and inbox interfaces.
- `scheduling` — Dates, calendars, and bookings.
- `productivity` — Task and workflow management.
- `content` — Editorial and informational blocks.
- `settings` — Configuration and preferences.
